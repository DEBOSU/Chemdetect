03 — HOW IT WORKS

How the Reaction Works

↗

From a Primary Alcohol to an Aldehyde

Methanol is a primary alcohol. Under partial oxidation, it can be converted into methanal, the simplest aldehyde.

  1. 01

    Reactant

    The reaction starts with methanol (CH₃OH).

  2. 02

    Oxidation

    The chemical structure of the functional group changes during oxidation.

  3. 03

    Methanal Formation

    In partial oxidation, methanal (HCHO) is the target product.

  4. 04

    Possible Further Oxidation

    Depending on the reaction conditions, oxidation may continue to form formic acid or other products.

i

SCIENTIFIC NOTE

A Reaction Is Not the Same as a Detection Test

Forming methanal from methanol explains a chemical pathway, but does not by itself prove that methanol is present in an unknown sample. Detection depends on the test used and its selectivity. The reagent name and actual test result should be stated when available.
